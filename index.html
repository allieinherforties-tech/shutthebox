How It Works
1

Roll and add. Roll two dice on every turn. Their total is your target.

2

Tap any tiles that sum to the roll, then close them.

3

Keep rolling. Your turn ends when no tiles can make your roll.

4

Score is the tiles left over — lower is better. Close them all to Shut the Box.

Once tiles 7, 8 and 9 are down, you may roll a single die instead of two.
